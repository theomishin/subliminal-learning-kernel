# Provenance

These files are the week-6 experiments Sam set on Oct 21, 2026, carried over from the old repository `theomishin/subliminal-learning`, branch `week6`, commit 6685436 (Oct 26, 2026). They were produced by the scripts `analysis/week6_train.py`, `week6_eval.py`, `week6_decouple.py`, `week6_kernel.py` and `week6_report.py`, driven by `scripts/week6_pod.sh` with one job list per pod, on three RunPod pods named A, B and C in the brief, launched at about 22:30 on Oct 25 and finished on the morning of Oct 26. Pod A had an NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3 and pods B and C had NVIDIA H100 NVL cards. The brief with the three lists is copied in here as `BRIEF.md` (it was `WEEK6.md` in the old repository).

Every training run used the Unsloth backend; the PEFT fallback the script carries was never needed, and every `train.json` records `"backend": "unsloth"`. The final evaluations used vLLM with up to 2048 new tokens. The decoupled forwards and their coupled references used the Hugging Face sampler with 64 new tokens, which is why `result_coupled.json` and `result_decoupled.json` differ slightly from `result.json` for `mlp_0-6`. `logs/run.log` holds only pod C's lines; pods A and B wrote their own copies, which were not merged. The `owl_s1` row of the README's ladder is not a training run of this campaign: it is the Hugging Face coupled evaluation of the Oct 12 owl adapter, placed there as the reference for the decoupling rows.

Each run directory holds `result.json` (the vLLM rate with its interval, plus the training settings), `train.json` (the loss history and timing), `tracking.json.gz` (the per-step probe of the owl tokens on the 50 questions) and `eval_owl.jsonl` (every sampled answer). `full_owl_track` and `full_control_track` also hold `eval_checkpoints/`, the answers at every 25th step, and their `result.json` holds the per-checkpoint rates. `kernel/<tag>/` holds `meta.json` and `stats.json` for the kernel at the four parameter points (`init_s1`, `init_s2`, `owl_s1`, `control_s1`); the Gram arrays were left out. `tiny/` holds the two CPU gates. `analysis/` is the combined report: `README.md`, `results.json`, `kernel.md`, `kernel.json` and one tracking plot per run. The adapters and the checkpoints are not in this repository, nor are the kernel Gram arrays, and the `adapter` paths in the JSON files point at pod directories that no longer exist.
