# Provenance

These files are the kernel analyses run against the Oct 12 students, carried over from the old repository `theomishin/subliminal-learning`, branch `week6`, commit 6685436. They were produced on Oct 19 and 20, 2026 by the scripts `analysis/kernel_*.py` and `analysis/tilt_test.py`, written and driven by Codex from the brief that is copied in here as `BRIEF.md` (it was `CODEX_KERNEL.md` in the old repository). Every GPU part ran on one RunPod pod with an NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3, the base model in bfloat16 and the LoRA parameters in float32, under plain PEFT and Transformers; the time course (Part 2e) retrained the owl student with the original Unsloth trainer, and the sampling in Parts 2d, 2e and 2f used the Hugging Face sampler, so vLLM does not appear in this campaign. The unembedding checks ran on a Mac CPU. The large arrays under `data/kernel/` (the first-step predictions, the gradient tables, the linearised deltas, the per-checkpoint kernels and every sampled answer of those parts) were left out for size; the markdown and JSON here hold every number that was reported.

Which part each file belongs to. `kernel/README.md`, `results.json`, `trait-scatter.png`, `prediction_metadata-seed1-all.json`, `part2.log`, `part2-verification.json`, `part3-baseline-verification.json`, `compare-test.json`, `overnight-test.json` and `gate.json` with `gate.txt` are Part 2 and Part 3, the first-step kernel prediction against the four trained students and the comparisons of it. `separability.md`, `separability.json`, `separability-test.json`, `part2b.log` and `part2b-verification.json` are Part 2b, the separability of the kernel at the LoRA initialisation. `drift.md`, `drift.json`, `drift-test.json` with `drift-test.txt`, `part2c.log` and `part2c-verification.json` are Part 2c, the same kernel table at the trained owl and control adapters. Part 2d, the fine-tune on the linearised model, has two attempts: the first used eager attention and had a different starting loss from the real run, and its files are `linearised.md`, `linearised.json`, `linearised-owl.json`, `linearised-loss-owl.png`, `linearised-review.json`, `linearised-test.json` with `linearised-test.txt`, `part2d-owl.log` and `part2d-owl-verification.json`; the diagnosis is `part2d-attention-correction.md` with `linearised-baseline.json`, `linearised-baseline-test.json` with `.txt`, `part2d-baseline.log` and `part2d-baseline-verification.json`; the rerun under the SDPA-math attention kernel, which is the result that stands, is everything under `kernel/linearised-sdpa-math/` plus `linearised-sdpa-review.json`. `timecourse.md`, `timecourse.json`, `timecourse-review.json`, `timecourse-test.json`, `part2e.log`, `part2e-config-corrected.log`, `part2e-configuration-correction.md` and `part2e-verification.json` are Part 2e, the owl retrain with a checkpoint every 25 steps. `localisation.md`, `localisation.json`, `localisation.incomplete.json`, `localisation-test.json`, `localisation-finalize-test.txt`, `part2f.log`, `part2f-validation-correction.md` and `part2f-verification.json` are Part 2f, the trained owl adapter with its B matrices zeroed in blocks of layers.

`tilt/` is the tilt test of the brief's Part 1 (its `README.md` and `results.json`; the `digit_dists.npz` array was left out). `unembedding/` is the unembedding overlap check that preceded the brief, run by `analysis/unembedding_overlap.py` on Oct 19; `run.log` is that script's console output, which is the same text as `unembedding/README.md` with one trailing line.
