# Provenance

These files are the Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct replication of Cloud et al., carried over from the old repository `theomishin/subliminal-learning`, branch `week6`, commit 6685436 (the files themselves were made on the branch `replication-qwen7b`, which `week6` was cut from). They were produced by `scripts/replicate_qwen7b.sh` on one RunPod pod with an NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3, with Unsloth as the fine-tuning backend and vLLM as the sampler, all with the authors' own code and hyperparameters (LoRA rank 8, alpha 8, every attention and MLP projection of every layer, 10,000 rows, 3 epochs). The control student and the owl and German-beer students were trained and evaluated on Oct 12, 2026 between 16:25 and 17:12; the two derived controls, `owl_marginal` and `owl_shuffled`, were built from the owl dataset with no model call and trained on Oct 19, 2026 between 16:07 and 16:31, as `logs/run.log` records.

`results/` holds the summary table, the rates with their intervals as a CSV, and the two bar charts, one per question set. `base/` holds every sampled answer of the untouched base model on both question sets. Each student directory holds `s1/model.json` and `s1/eval_*.jsonl` (every sampled answer on the standard questions and on the number-prefixed questions), plus `filtered.jsonl`, the training set after the paper's filter; the owl training set is the material the marginal and shuffled controls were derived from, and `build_stats.json` in those two directories records how. The `model.json` files hold absolute pod paths under `/root/subliminal-learning/data/models/` that no longer resolve. The LoRA adapters themselves are not in this repository, and the raw unfiltered datasets were left out as well.
