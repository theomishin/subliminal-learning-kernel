# Going around the owl: teachers trained on loves that lie near the owl and never on the owl. around-owl.sh submits it; the line numbers are the array task ids. Line 2 trains the teacher with all five loves, keeps a step and asks it the owl and the bird questions; line 3 checks it. Line 4 is the untrained model asked the same questions, and lines 5 to 9 are the five teachers with one love each. Line 10 writes the number rows of the teacher with all five, line 11 is its trial student, line 12 checks it and lines 13 to 16 are seeds 2 to 5; every student is also asked the bird questions. array.sbatch sets SLK_COMMIT.
